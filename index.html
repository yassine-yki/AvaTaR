COMMON QUESTIONS

What you need to know.

What can Avatar AI automate?+

We focus on repetitive, information-heavy workflows: sorting and summarizing messages, preparing responses, extracting information from documents, and connecting steps across your existing tools.

Is the automation customized for my business?+

Yes. We begin with your actual process, rules, tools, and approval requirements, then build the smallest reliable workflow that solves the problem.

Will the AI take actions without approval?+

Consequential actions remain under human control. We design clear review steps and permissions around the risk level of each workflow.

Does the Email Assistant send replies automatically?+

No. It prepares an editable suggestion, but it never sends, deletes, archives, or modifies email automatically.

How is business data protected?+

Our approach includes least-privilege access, encrypted credentials, separated customer data, limited retention, and auditable workflow activity.
