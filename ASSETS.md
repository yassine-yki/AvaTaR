# Visuels et prompts

Les images servent d’illustrations. Elles ne représentent pas les locaux, l’équipe ou l’infrastructure réelle d’Avatar AI.

## Génération intégrée OpenAI

Mode utilisé : outil intégré `image_gen` (pas de CLI ni de clé API utilisateur). Les originaux générés sont conservés. Les copies JPEG du site conservent le cadrage et les dimensions d’origine ; seule la compression a été optimisée.

### `assets/silicon-hero.jpg`

Prompt :

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: full-bleed website hero for an AI automation studio. Create a cinematic macro photograph of a large raw metallic silicon crystal, fragmented angular jagged facets, dark graphite and silver mineral texture, photographed like a precious scientific material, on a perfectly plain cool medium gray seamless background. Landscape 16:9 composition. The crystalline mass fills the lower 85% of the image, rises toward the center-right and extends beyond the bottom and right crop, with calm gray negative space in the upper left for a small white title and dark lower left area suitable for white brand typography. Fine physical imperfections and crystalline surface detail, soft directional studio light, restrained realistic contrast, museum-quality material study. No letters, no logos, no text, no watermark, no abstract glowing lines, no neon, no futuristic UI. This is raw silicon as a metaphor for intelligence and computational potential, not a product or specific facility. Save a high quality landscape photograph.

### `assets/workplace.jpg`

Prompt :

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: full-screen website photography for an AI automation company, landscape 16:9. A low-angle architectural photograph of a modern minimalist office building, brushed silver metal mullions and reflective blue glass grid rising from the bottom right into a vivid clear blue sky with a few wispy white clouds. Expansive empty blue sky across the upper left 50% for white heading. Strong geometric composition and daylight, subtle analog grain, realistic architectural editorial photography. Building occupies the lower half and right, image fills screen. No people, no logos, no signs, no text. Calm, confident, tangible workplace potential. Not a literal company office, illustrative architecture.

### `assets/collaboration.jpg`

Prompt :

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: full-screen editorial website backdrop, landscape 16:9. Inside a very dark contemporary office, viewed from a distance: a small group of three anonymous colleagues in silhouette, standing and discussing beside enormous floor-to-ceiling windows overlooking a bright deep blue sky and distant city. Architectural composition: black ceiling and dark wall fill upper 45% of frame with uninterrupted negative space for white website heading, windows span lower 55%, silhouettes small at the bottom edge, luminous blue daylight beyond. Photojournalistic and cinematic, natural proportions, quiet human collaboration, realistic no neon no holograms. No faces identifiable, no logos, no text, no watermark. Illustrative scene, not a real named team.
